# Ticket 09 design ruling (advisor AdvisorT09, 2026-10-15)

Ruling on `local://t09-brief.md` (DesignT09, at `main` db85ebb; at the close both land under `.scratch/dwarf-harness/c2-evidence/` as `ticket-09-design-brief.md` and `ticket-09-design-ruling.md`). Where they differ, this ruling wins. Under D117 it ends the design step: no owner design question; the build starts from the brief as modified here, after ticket 08 closes. The owner's freeze of F2-09 (OG-F9) comes before the code that writes it. Abbreviations as the brief's (T09, SPEC, CORE, RC, DEC, LD, LAW, UC, CON, WD, R8, F8, BUD, ENV, BR = the brief); bare Go names are `internal/core/workspace/`.

**Verdict:** approve-with-changes.

The brief is faithful to CORE:868–872 (B5's durable one-off activation), to D64–D69 as D77 amends them, to the three criteria (T09:28–30), to spec story 19–21 and testing decision 10 (SPEC:65–69, :609), to the manifest tuple of UC:456–458 and LAW:242–248, and to ticket 08 as ruled and frozen (R8; F8/D123). Of the 18 choices, 11 are accepted, 6 modified and 1 rejected. The two major changes both remove something: (1) `policy_approval_withdrawn`, `withdrawActivationApproval` and the deferred `rejected/authority_stale` go; they are a C2 stand-in for C3's authority revocation (F3 owns approval records, CORE:844), a second, non-CAS way to end the same request beside CAS cancellation, and a two-step state with its own crash row; in C2 the owner's authorization is the pending request itself, valid until cancelled or replaced, and reauthorization is a new request (P3). (2) A stale `expected_active_record_hash` is refused at admission, as `UpdateInputs` refuses a stale revision, not admitted and then durably rejected `manifest_stale`: with one pending slot per colony and only `policy_activated` changing the manifest, the active manifest cannot change between a request's admission and its due time, so the brief's live rejection path is reachable only by admitting a request known to be doomed, which then occupies the slot and holds affected workspaces for nothing (P18). With both, `ended` loses `rejected` and `reason`, and F2-09 is four types plus the optional intent key. The minor changes narrow the bootstrap scope and key the new-intent rule to the first activation (P4), drop the regressed-clock hint timer (P7), drop the redundant recovery-phase switch condition (P8/P11), open `ended.outcome` to F2-06's token grammar and make `observed_utc_ns` a conditional plain `int64` (P12), and drop the loader re-run at the switch (P2). Owner gates: OG-F9 confirmed, with its packet consequences below; nothing else in the brief is the owner's. Slicing: S1 record-free and routine after ticket 08's close; S2 may start now in `contract/ledger` and merges only with the freeze; S3a and S3b follow it.

## Owner gates

- **OG-F9** (the owner's): freeze F2-09 by addition to `ledger-format` version 1 (D40, D113) through a one- or two-page packet after S2 is built, evidenced and reviewed here, before S3a writes a record (D116 item 7); the same answer is D41's sign-off for S2 reaching `main`, which covers the optional key on frozen `finalization_intended` as OG-F8 covered `generation_derived`'s; its decision entry and the `CONTRACTS.md` register change land with S2 (D116 item 3). The packet's choices: the four names, the open `outcome` token, the conditional `observed_utc_ns`, the signed UTC ns fields, `owner_approval_id`, the derived `affected_workspace_ids`, the intent key and its presence rule, and the golden of finding 9. Consequences, one line each: approved bytes are retained for the life of the log and never unpinned; check and environment bytes get identity, not meaning; the request is the owner's exact approval, ended only by CAS cancellation or replacement, and reapproval is a new request; one pending request per colony, revision-checked; durable request-owned clock and due holds, an untrusted clock holding rather than bypassing; one actual-time colony-wide switch whose sequence and identity come from the envelope; after the first activation a workspace's new intents bind the active manifest and an unconfigured workspace cannot intend; replay never executes; the product still answers `no_validator`.
- **Not the owner's:** no D29 dependency; no D52 change (nothing admits, validates or moves `main` through the product); no D42 change; no D117 question; the B-NEXT entry at the close. Conditional: D122 if a race step exceeds 15 minutes. The C2 status change at the close is the drafter's under D116 item 4, made only when C2's roadmap prerequisites are actually met.
