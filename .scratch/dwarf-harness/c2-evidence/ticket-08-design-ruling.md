# Ticket 08 design ruling (advisor AdvisorT08, 2026-10-13)

Ruling on the design brief `local://t08-brief.md` (DesignT08; at the ticket's close both land under `.scratch/dwarf-harness/c2-evidence/` as `ticket-08-design-brief.md` and `ticket-08-design-ruling.md`). The owner's freeze of F2-08 (OG-F8) comes before the code that writes it. Sources are cited as in the brief: RC = `docs/ROADMAP-CORE.md`, CORE = `docs/DESIGN-CORE-AND-LAUNCHERS.md`, DEC = `docs/DECISIONS.md`, LD = `contract/ledger/doc.go`, WD = `internal/core/workspace/doc.go`, CON = `docs/CONTRACTS.md`, UC = `docs/DESIGN-UNDERCURRENT.md`, SPEC = `.scratch/dwarf-harness/spec.md`, T08 = the ticket, B6/R6 = ticket 06's brief and ruling, R7/N7 = ticket 07's ruling and S3 notes, AN = `local://t07-notes-from-t06.md`, BR = the brief, SUP = `local://t08-code-supplement.md`, probes = `local://t08-git-probes.md`; bare Go file names are `internal/core/workspace/`; all at `main` 702ca66.

**Verdict:** approve-with-changes.

The brief is faithful to the approved boundary (RC:278–416; CORE:850–882; D112), to ticket 08's five criteria (T08:28–32), to D55, D57, D59, D71 and D77 as the map quotes them, to B23–B25 and ticket 07's ruling, and the probes support the guarded CAS, the backend check and the directory-flush recipe. Of the 22 choices, 14 are accepted, 7 modified and 1 rejected. The three major changes are all to the records, and all three remove something: (1) `workspace.canonical_anchored` goes; the witness chain starts at the first `promotion_accepted`, and `predecessor_witness_seq` is optional, present exactly when a prior acceptance exists, because a Git read made durable at the first finalization is not an accepted predecessor and buys no proof the intent's own bindings do not give (P3); (2) the evidence reference (three required keys, a copied `body_refs`, a pin and a `fixture.` type) goes; it is a placeholder payload for F4 (CORE:844; RC:220), and evidence and revision bindings join by addition when their producers freeze them, as F2-06 deferred attempt IDs (R6 P14) (P4); (3) the `outcome_rules` marker and `intent_id` on `promotion_failed` go; the rules for `cancelled` and `interrupted` are conditional on the token, F2-07's precedent for `temporary_merged_back` (LD:765–766), so no second convention and no decoder change (P21). The other modifications confine the writer-quiescence proof to an unresolved intent, make a reopened workspace with live requests refuse work until the bookkeeping phase ends them (D55), keep one fsync recipe, record `changed_paths` once, and collapse the proof structs to what the records bind. Owner gates: OG-F8 is confirmed, with the consequences listed below in its packet; nothing else in the brief is the owner's. Slicing: S1 is record-free and routine after ticket 07's close; S2 may start now in `contract/ledger` and merges only with the freeze; S3a and S3b follow it.
