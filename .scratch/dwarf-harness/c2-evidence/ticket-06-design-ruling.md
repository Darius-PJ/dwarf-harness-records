# Ticket 06 design ruling (advisor AdvisorT06, 2026-10-12)

Ruling on the design brief `local://t06-brief.md` (DesignT06; at the ticket's close both land under `.scratch/dwarf-harness/c2-evidence/` as `ticket-06-design-brief.md` and `ticket-06-design-ruling.md`). Where the two differ, this ruling wins. The owner's approval of the design (OG-A6) comes before any code; his freeze of F2-06 (OG-F6) comes before the code that writes it. Sources are cited as in the brief: RC = `docs/ROADMAP-CORE.md`, CORE = `docs/DESIGN-CORE-AND-LAUNCHERS.md`, DEC = `docs/DECISIONS.md`, LD = `contract/ledger/doc.go`, WD = `internal/core/workspace/doc.go`, T0n = the tickets, RU = ticket 05's ruling, G6-Mn = `local://t06-git-probe.md`, all at `main` 8c2c328.

**Verdict:** approve-with-changes.

The brief is faithful to the approved boundary (RC:278–416, CORE:850–882, D112), to ticket 06's four criteria (T06:28–31), to B23/B24 as built and to the 06/07/08/09 split; every source I read says what the brief claims, and probes M1–M9 support the integration recipe. All 25 choices are accepted, six of them with modifications; none is rejected. One major change: a conflict report is a body that Core's failure obligation needs, so CORE:882 ("pin bodies needed for active finalization/failure obligation") requires it pinned with its generation's commit, released on supersession or at the scratch's closure, never left to the owner's collection while the context has not acted on it; F2-06's `generation_derived` gains a `report_pin_id` and the closure commit gains the matching unpins. The other modifications close two check-then-use windows (the head rule and the live-request rule re-checked under the scratch exclusion, D62's "serialize this admission check before creating an additional snapshot, including when submissions compete"), place the closure rule inside F2-06 rather than on F2-05's text, and complete the owner summary (both directions of ignored tree attributes; queued requests waiting behind a conflict; conflict reports kept until closure). Owner gates: OG-A6 and OG-F6 are confirmed; P10, P17 and P19 need no separate question and belong in OG-A6's summary (P17 also in OG-F6's packet). Slicing: S1 is record-free and routine after OG-A6; S2 reaches `main` only with the freeze; S3 is routine after it.
