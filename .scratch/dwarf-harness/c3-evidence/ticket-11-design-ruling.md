# Ticket 11 design ruling (advisor AdvisorT11, 2026-10-23)

Ruling on `local://t11-brief.md` (DesignerT11, at `main` 1e6c3dd; at the close both land under `.scratch/dwarf-harness/c3-evidence/` as `ticket-11-design-brief.md` and `ticket-11-design-ruling.md`). Where they differ, this ruling wins; the frozen F0/F2/F3-10 win over both on encodings. Under D117 it ends the design step: no owner design question; S1 may start now. The PM's freeze of F3-11 (D125) comes before the code that writes it. Abbreviations as the brief's (T11, SPEC, RC, CORE, DEC, CON, LD, AS, LS, R10, BR = the brief).

**Verdict:** approve-with-changes.

The brief is faithful to T11:29–34, D82/B7/B9, CORE:918–930, RC:433–437/:444–450/:492, D21–D23, D37 and ticket 10's ruling (P2, P7–P9). Of the 17 choices, 7 are accepted and 10 modified; none is rejected. The modifications remove things or move them out of the contract: (1) numeric caps and D22's per-agent setting leave the F3-11 decoders and become `Limits` (finding 2); (2) a `task.create` grant stops pre-naming each task ID, which was per-task consent (finding 1); (3) task audience becomes agent-level and submission/evidence references become a `SubjectRef` with an open kind so ticket 12 extends by addition (findings 3–4); (4) `lifetime` becomes an open, fail-closed token (finding 5); (5) owner `StartTurn` goes (finding 7); (6) `unknown` counts as the ending record that matches a start, so D37 holds (finding 8). On the weighted question, P13 does not break F3-10 and is not an owner gate: no F3-10 byte, decoder rule, group shape, actor or golden changes, `decodeGroup` stays the F3-10 validator and never sees an F3-11 record (AS/service.go:224–226; AS/replay.go:28–41), and no log valid today becomes invalid. What changes is outside F3-10's text: its "current scope revision" and transcript "exact previous head" (LD:1627–1633) are henceforth advanced by two types, so a consumer declaring only F3-10 consumption cannot reconstruct the private heads of a log holding F3-11 — it refuses the next owner revision as stale (AS/replay.go:171–173) or its predecessor (AS/replay.go:188–190), and where no owner revision follows it would hold an older head. The freeze record states this (finding 10). It is the additive-type case D139 anticipated ("new types or versions through their own freeze records"), recorded as this ruling's reading, originator `assistant`. The owner-only alternative — reusing `private.revision` inside an F3-11 group — would carve into F3-10's group-shape and actor rules (LD:1653–1663) and is worse. All ten types are needed by criteria 1, 3, 5 and 6 (D88's rework, T11 comment of 2026-10-08) and D37; none is ticket 12–16's.

## Owner gates

- **None applies now.** OG-D52 untriggered (D112 approved the boundary before C3; nothing admits, validates or moves canonical; every workload is a test). OG-BREAK untriggered (F3-11 is additive; F3-10's bytes, rules and golden unchanged; the shared private head is this ruling's additive reading, finding 10). OG-DEP untriggered (standard library plus `contract/ledger`, its appender, `ledgerstore`, `durable`; no module).
- **Not the owner's:** the F3-11 freeze (the PM under D125 once this advisor approves S2 with its D-NEXT record and evidence; that approval is D41's sign-off for S2); the `Limits` defaults including the per-agent 3 (D22 calls it a setting; the C3 workload gate measures it); the shared-head reading (originator `assistant`); C3's Status, unchanged.
- **Missed by the brief:** nothing the documents reserve to him. Two items to record at the close, not gates: (1) a dispatched local work left `unknown` has no resolution path in ticket 11 — ticket 15's executor supplies the proven ending; (2) agents cannot create their own work contexts (F3-10 freezes creation as an owner actor), so a claim needs an owner-created unbound context until a later producer adds agent-originated creation by a new type.
