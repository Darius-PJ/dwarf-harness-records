# Ticket 142 evidence

The [design brief](ticket-142-design-brief.md) and [ruling](ticket-142-design-ruling.md) govern the ticket; the [step-2 profile summary](ticket-142-profile-summary.md) is the attribution run the design used. Ticket 142's Answer and the `internal/core/agents` package documentation state what was built. No decision entry is added (D161). The ruling's amendment 10 asked for an assistant entry recording that its checkpoint publication rule replaces D159's ticket-140 two-site rule; the PM decided against it because `CURRENT-RULES.md` lists D159 as history only, so no standing rule changes, and ReviewT142S2 confirmed this (note 8). The rule, recorded here instead: a token may be published only by (a) a successful `openService` at `openedThrough` from its own replay; (b) a successful nonduplicate contiguous scoped acknowledgement at the new frontier from its own `readCommittedRange`; (c) a refresh that reached numeric EOF at T with successful close; (d) a `Since` page that reached T, or its proof pass that reached E, with successful close; (e) the review path's readback when it reaches EOF with successful close. Every publication requires terminal EOF, successful actual Close and a token returned by that Reader; errors, equal bounds, `ToEnd` and early stops publish nothing. ReviewT142S2 ruled that S2's locked post-`Submit` acknowledgement readback meets condition (c) and adds no publication condition, so it is not a sixth site (S2 answer 1).
