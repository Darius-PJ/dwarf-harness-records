# Ticket 10 design ruling (advisor AdvisorT10, 2026-10-21)

Ruling on `local://t10-brief.md` (DesignT10, at `main` 976e19b; at the close both land under `.scratch/dwarf-harness/c3-evidence/` as `ticket-10-design-brief.md` and `ticket-10-design-ruling.md`). Where they differ, this ruling wins; the frozen F3-10 wins over both on encodings. Under D117 it ends the design step: no owner design question; S1 may start now. The PM's freeze of F3-10 (D125) comes before the code that writes it. Abbreviations as the brief's (T10, SPEC, RC, CORE, DEC, CON, LD, BR = the brief); `WS` = `internal/core/workspace/`, `LS` = `internal/core/ledgerstore/`; bare Go names are the new package's.

**Verdict:** approve-with-changes.

The brief is faithful to CORE:918 and RC:421/:483 (bytes before the `private.revision` event, mismatch reported not guessed), RC:450 (the receipt tuple), SPEC:393–395 and :403, T10:29–33, D21/D25/D37/B7, and to the frozen F0 command rules (LD:201–217; LS/commands.go:131–145). Of the 14 choices, 7 are accepted, 6 modified and 1 rejected. The three major changes each remove something. (1) P7's admission/completion split goes: a private write publishes its content-addressed bytes, which are unreferenced until the commit and have no external effect, then commits one group, exactly as WS's `UpdateInputs` does (register.go:115–166); `private_write_started`/`_ended`, `ReconcileInterrupted` and the pending/interrupted private operation are a second convention beside that one, and criterion 3 holds by construction once the only durable facts are complete groups. (2) P2's `OwnerControl`/`ContextAccess` go: in process they prove possession of the live `*Service`, which the host that called `Open` already has; what the designer actually wanted — no caller-stated `ledger.Actor`, a Core-derived principal, no agent entry point — stays. (3) P5/P9's permanent block on Core's own commit-before-materialize gap goes: a materialization whose bytes equal an earlier committed object of its slot (or is absent) is `stale` and is completed from the verified head object at Open and reported; only `foreign` bytes or a missing/corrupt object block. Without (3) the brief's own Windows sharing-violation row wedges a scope forever on the first-release platform. F3-10 shrinks to four types; the golden becomes 10 commits / 34 records.

## Owner gates

- **None applies now.** The brief's conditional gates stand untriggered: OG-D52 (nothing admits, validates or moves canonical through the product; D112 approved the boundary before C3), OG-BREAK (additions only; the four types join `ledger-format` version 1 by addition) and OG-DEP (standard library plus `contract/ledger`, `contract/ledger/appender`, `internal/core/ledgerstore`, `internal/core/durable`; no module).

- **Missed by the brief:** nothing the documents reserve to the owner. One item to record at the close, not a gate: a `foreign` materialization or a missing object stays blocked with no repair path in ticket 10; ticket 16 or a later entry supplies the authorized repair.
