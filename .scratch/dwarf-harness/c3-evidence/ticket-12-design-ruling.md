# Ticket 12 design ruling (advisor AdvisorT12Ruling, 2026-10-24)

Ruling on `local://t12-brief.md` (DesignerT12, at `main` d7ed4ef; at the close both land under `.scratch/dwarf-harness/c3-evidence/` as `ticket-12-design-brief.md` and `ticket-12-design-ruling.md`). Where they differ, this ruling wins; the frozen F0/F2/F3-10/F3-11 win over both on encodings. Under D117 it ends the design step: no owner design question; S1 may start now. The PM's freeze of F3-12 (D125) comes before the code that writes it. Abbreviations: BR = the brief (line numbers are its), T12 = ticket 12, SPEC, RC = `docs/ROADMAP-CORE.md`, CORE = `docs/DESIGN-CORE-AND-LAUNCHERS.md`, DEC = `docs/DECISIONS.md`, LD = `contract/ledger/doc.go`, SV = `contract/ledger/scoped_values.go`, AS = `internal/core/agents`, LS = `internal/core/ledgerstore`, R11 = `ticket-11-design-ruling.md`, EV11 = `ticket-11-evidence.md`, T140 = ticket 140's draft (E2 in `local://t139-t140-publication-edits.md`), MAP = `local://t12-reader-code.md`.

**Verdict:** approve-with-changes.

The brief is faithful to T12:29–31 and :51, CORE:920, RC:447–450, D52, D82, D140 and R11 P7. Of the 15 choices, 9 are accepted and 6 modified; none is rejected. The modifications remove weight or cost: (1) `check`/`config` become bound digests, not artifact references that today only dummy tasks can supply (finding 1); (2) two result types become one `runtime.review_result` with a closed verdict (finding 3); (3) the callback rechecks current chains instead of requiring the colony `authority_version` unchanged (finding 4); (4) a fresh request proves its ID new by the `maxCommand` watermark discipline and source witnesses are read by the reader's cursor, so ticket 12 adds no new whole-history scan to the ordinary path (finding 2); (5) the direct-transition guard lands with the reducer in S3a (finding 5); (6) `request_sha256` goes (finding 8). P8/P11's separate family is additive under D40/D125 on D140 finding 10's precedent and is not OG-BREAK; its reading must be recorded (finding 6). No owner gate applies.

## Owner gates

- **None applies now.** OG-D52 untriggered (D112 approved the boundary; nothing promotes, validates or admits colony work; every subject kind that would is refused). OG-BREAK untriggered (F3-12 is a separate family; no F3-11 byte, rule, operation, group shape or golden changes; the task reading is this ruling's additive reading, finding 6, as D140 finding 10 was). OG-DEP untriggered (standard library plus the existing set; `time` is standard library).
- **Not the owner's:** the F3-12 freeze (the PM under D125 once this advisor approves S2 with its D-NEXT record and evidence); the `Limits` defaults; the clock seam; the guard binding him (within delegated design; he keeps every escape in P9).
- **Missed by the brief:** nothing the documents reserve to him. Items to record at the close under Limits, not gates: (1) after a reject, F3-11's frozen table has no submitted→active, so the ways on are replacement then approve/fail, cancel or reassign; a revise-and-resubmit path needs a later type version; (2) a pending failure request on an active task goes stale when the assignee submits or waits (its version moves); the requester re-requests; (3) a requester must hold the decision authority it delegates (P6); (4) the guard binds the owner and his escapes are P9's; (5) a backwards system clock refuses new review decisions until it passes the floor (P10).

## Slicing

- **S1** (record-free; routine review; `-race=false`): the slot state machine, binding comparison, the reviewed-failure kernel fact and `ErrReviewRequired` predicate at `planTask`, the sample/floor/deadline rule and the request-ID watermark, all on unexported agents values with pure tests; must not predict S2's Go type names (R11's S1 rule); existing entrances behave as today because no slot exists until S3a.
- **S2** (contract): three codecs, `private_revision`, the encoder fix, catalogue, schema-3 submissions, payload/compatibility/fuzz/golden tests and the f3/12 fixtures; register rows, D-NEXT freeze entry stating finding 6's reading; reviewed with record and evidence together; lands only with the PM's freeze. **Nothing writes F3-12 before it** (D113, D125).
- **S3a / S3b stay separate.** S3a is the reducer for all three types through `Open` and the golden, slot lifecycle, cold lookups, book usage, task loaders, consumption versions, the passive queries, **and the direct complete/fail guard** (finding 5): all of it is exercised by the golden and group-rule rows, none is dead. S3b is the four producers, clock, witnesses, CAS, Submit and races. Neither exports a stub. **S4** last with the race step (D129), fuzz of changed targets, `doc.go`, crash rows, labelled measurement. **Close** one Markdown-only commit (D128), never C3 Done.
