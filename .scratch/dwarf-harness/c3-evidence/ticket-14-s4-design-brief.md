## Proposed choices
- **P1. Recognize the TTL split, strictly.** In `input_tokens_details`, `cache_write_tokens_details` is a known member: an object whose known members are `ttl_30m_tokens` and `ttl_12h_tokens` (other members follow P3). `ttl_12h_tokens` must be absent, null or 0, since the pinned profile prices only 30-minute writes (ruling P4); a nonzero value holds with a profile violation (an unpriced class). When present, `ttl_30m_tokens + ttl_12h_tokens` must equal `cache_write_tokens`; otherwise the usage is inconsistent and holds with a violation. Pricing is unchanged: `cache_write_tokens` stays the write class's units.
- **P2. A new usage-schema version.** The change ships as `openai-responses-text-30m-kguard/v2`. Profiles with `/v1` keep v1's derivation exactly (refusing the member), so no recorded observation or the frozen chapter-14 golden changes meaning, as the ruling's P2 and P9 require ("never a reinterpretation of settled history"). The live profile is rebuilt with `/v2` (same rates, limits and quotes; a new profile digest), and the drill driver is unchanged. The new token is additive F3-13 vocabulary: the PM records a short D164 under D125, reviewed with the slice, with one sentence in `CONTRACTS.md`. The alternative, extending v1 in place, is simpler but changes how a v1 observation derives on late re-derivation, and the PM doesn't recommend it.
- **P3. Amendment 4 at any depth, under v2.** Under v2, an unknown member anywhere in `usage` whose value is null, plain 0, or an object whose every leaf is null or plain 0 does not hold; any nonzero or non-integer leaf or unknown shape still holds with a violation (unchanged).
- **P4. No golden change.** `/v2` is an open token value with no codec change; chapter 14 stays byte-identical. Proof is by unit vectors in `internal/core/providers` and one Core settlement row, not a new golden chapter, unless the ruling requires one.
- **P5. Tests (D152; money rule breaks).** A byte fixture of the live-shaped terminal (sanitized: the shape file's usage plus synthetic IDs) settles under v2 at 4 micros (ceil(9 × 1/10 + 5 × 1/2) = ceil(3.4)) and holds with a violation under v1. Vectors: a consistent nonzero 30-minute split settles at the write rate; nonzero `ttl_12h_tokens` holds with a violation; a split that disagrees with `cache_write_tokens` holds with a violation; an unknown nested all-zero object settles under v2 and holds under v1; an unknown nested nonzero leaf holds. One Core row through `PrepareOpenAI`…`RecordPaidObservation` on loopback SSE with a v2 profile settles 4 micros. Rule breaks: (a) accept a nonzero `ttl_12h_tokens`; (b) skip the sum check; (c) apply v2's tolerance under v1. Fuzz: extend `FuzzOpenAIResponsesSSE` and `FuzzProviderProfile` seeds; the PM runs 120 s campaigns.
- **P6. Slice.** One slice, S4, in `internal/core/providers` (usage, profile validation of the new token) and test-only `internal/core/agents` (one settlement row), on `main` once S1–S3 merge. No production change in `agents` unless the profile validation there lists usage-schema tokens. Then the owner's one further live attempt with the v2 profile.
