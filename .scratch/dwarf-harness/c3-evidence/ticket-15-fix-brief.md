## 1. Defect A — two writers, one ledger: a producer commits a group its own reducer refuses (D152: integrity)
The agents `Service` and the turn loop's `workspace.Service` both append to the one ledger store. `Service.mu` serializes only agents producers. A producer refreshes, guards and preflights against its projection, then `store.Submit` appends at whatever the tail has become; a workspace commit (a turn-end `CommitObserved`/`MergeBack`, `loop_manifest.go:272,278`, or a host-side workspace action) can land in between. The group is then judged at its real position:
- paid admissions and dispatch intents carry a clock sample (`AdmissionUTCNS`, `ObservedUTCNS`) below the review floor the foreign commit raised (`replay.go:483-485`) → `paid_attempt.go:139-140` `ErrDenied` → `invalid paid transition: agents: denied` (the measurement's error);
- loop groups carry a predicted receipt `EffectSequence` (`loop_journal.go:21,109`) → `loop_replay.go:81-82` refuses `loop effect or sequence`.
The post-Submit refresh poisons the service and the bad group is durable, so `Open` fails at it. Reproduced deterministically (`t15-diag-evidence\zz_t15_diag_interleave_test.go`, 4b1a0996…; red on 3a66a3c and on S3's 3c10c90; S4 not involved). The review producer has the same latent floor hazard (`review_execute.go:104,139-140`, `review_plan.go:142`), untested. Scoped/private groups and paid results tolerate a gap (they re-read their committed range or carry no clock or predicted position).

**Proposed fix (the diagnostic's option 1):** a conditional submit on Core's concrete writer: `ledgerstore` gains a method that appends only if the durable tail equals the caller's expected tail, and otherwise refuses with a typed error that writes nothing and uses no sequence (the frozen `appender` contract is untouched: its doc reserves such additions for "Core's concrete writer, as a separate method or interface"). `submitPaidLocked` (paid admit and dispatch intent), `commitLoop` and the review producer pass `s.p.through`; on the refusal they refresh and redo their guards from the top (paid resamples its clock; the loop re-predicts), a small fixed number of times, then return a busy error: the loop parks the turn as `waiting_resource` (woken by the next commit), and a direct owner or host caller gets the typed busy. No F3-13/F3-15/F3-12 encoding, reducer rule or golden changes. A retry happens before any send (the intent is not yet committed), so nothing is re-dispatched.
Considered and not proposed: an exclusive store writer turn held from refresh through submit (lock-order risk with the workspace, and workspace commits would wait out full-history scans); serializing only the loop's own workspace writes (host-side workspace commits still interleave).

## 2. Defect B — a busy workspace capture strands a finished turn (D152: data loss of the ending)
At turn end, `finish()` turns any error from `CommitObserved` into `recovery_pending` (`loop_manifest.go:213-222`) and the scheduler deletes the turn without `loop.end` or a native ending (`loop_runtime.go:358-361`). Three turns finishing together exhaust the workspace's `BytesInFlight` and the third gets `workspace: busy: capture_budget` (3 of 7 runs at 3×0×1). This contradicts `agents/doc.go:882-884` ("Busy from a local or paid entrance parks the turn as waiting_resource"); nothing wakes the loop on a workspace release today.

**Proposed fix:** a workspace `BusyError` at `finish` parks the turn as `waiting_resource` (its paid and tool work is durable; only the capture, the merge-back and the ending remain) and retries the finish when a workspace reservation is released; deadline, cancellation and shutdown keep their §2.1 precedence while it is parked. Proof: several writers finishing together with `BytesInFlight` equal to one capture's `MaxBytes`; on 3a66a3c one turn is stranded without `loop.end`, after the fix all finish.
