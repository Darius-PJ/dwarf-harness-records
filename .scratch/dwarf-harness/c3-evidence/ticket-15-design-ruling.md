# Ticket 15 design ruling (advisor, 2026-10-30)

Ruling on `local://t15-brief.md` (DesignT15, base aa81bb2). Where they differ this ruling wins; F3-10/11/12/13/139 win over both. Under D117/D160 it ends the design step: S1 and S2 may start now; S3 waits for the F3-15 freeze (D125); S4 waits for S3.

**Verdict: approve-with-amendments.** All ten choices stand; P1, P2, P3, P5, P7 and P9 are amended. Nothing frozen breaks; F3-15 is an additive six-type family the PM freezes after S2. Of the brief's claims I checked against code, two needed correction and neither is a reject: `DispatchPaid` returns `paid_active` busy *after* `AdmitPaid` has installed a durable hold (`paid_dispatch.go:70`, `paid_admit.go:60`), so the brief's `waiting_resource` wording would leave reserved holds without a send path under exchange saturation (amendment 1); and `workspace.promotion.request` has no F3-11 grant operation (`contract/ledger/doc.go:1845-1852`), so the catalogue entry authorized by `turn.inspect` plus scratch ownership infers permission from an unrelated grant (amendment 4).

## Compatibility and the schema-6 question
Schema-6 is a **new receipt type beside the frozen ones**: `runtime.loop_receipt` embeds the schema-6 submission exactly as `scoped_receipt`/`review_receipt`/`paid_receipt`/`registration_receipt` embed schemas 2–5. F3-11's closed schema-2 operation list (`doc.go:2055-2062`) and `runtime.scoped_receipt` are untouched; F3-15 groups embed no older-family record, as F3-11 did for F3-10 (`doc.go:1757`). Earlier goldens stay byte-identical and replay unchanged through `Open`. `private.turn_revision` joins the private-head chain on the F3-11 precedent (`doc.go:1751-1757`; ticket-11 ruling finding at line 67): recorded reading, not a byte change. `tool_started` as a turn child is the F3-13 finding-2 pattern. `LocalAdmission.ArgumentsSHA256` is an opaque digest in F3-11; its preimage is an F3-15 producer rule. The providers grammar is record-free; ticket 13's P15 refused Opus/Sonnet prior-tool history "without ticket 15's thinking-artifact replay", so narrowing that refusal here reopens no built guarantee and changes no money rule.

## P7 against the supplement
Every unverified item fails closed: missing-`tool_result` status (locally every call gets a result); "verbatim" (stored assembled bytes replayed once; a provider 400 is a held unknown attempt, no retry — recorded as a limit, loopback cannot prove acceptance); Haiku 5.5 (not configured); OpenAI omission/unmatched/order/aggregate (every item preserved, every call matched, `parallel_tool_calls:false`, 64 KiB results). None becomes a P-choice.

## Money
Each model call is one sealed, separately admitted attempt with a full inclusive hold; tool definitions, tool-use prompt, replayed history and retained thinking count once in input (B9); no second wallet; no automatic resend; the turn counters bound dispatch, never authorize spend. Amendment 1 closes the only gap: a hold must not be created where no send can proceed.

## Authority and disclosure
Host fixes principal/origin; discovery filters before paging and confers nothing; invocation resolves and rechecks after unlocked work and before disclosure; normalization binds `request_sha256` over normalized bytes incl. schema and target; corrections are static strings, zero model calls; unknown and inaccessible targets are indistinguishable. Amendment 5 keeps the authorizing chain in the record for passive disclosures that otherwise leave no grant evidence.

## Scheduler, continuation owner, live text
One scheduler goroutine, rings of ready agents/contexts, workers bounded by admitted turn slots, no goroutine per dormant context, admitted-or-busy with no retained start; criterion 7's order is §2.1 verbatim. D49 snapshot/high-water attach, bounded nonblocking subscriptions, `reattach_required` on overflow, provisional text never a durable final; attribution by bound IDs. Approved.

## Scope
Nothing of 16, 90, 91, C6, C7, R0/R1 or 39 is built; D23 wiring at turn end is C3's own deliverable (roadmap C3 "D23 wired to turns"), the drills stay 16's. No criterion is uncovered.

## Measurement
Criterion 8 records figures without bounds (no D160 profile). The declared workload is honest; amendment 8 adds the wait figures so loopback latencies under `MaxActivePaid=3` are not read as per-call latency.

## Owner gates
None required. One optional OG-LIVE (two keyed tool-loop drills) is offered because provider acceptance of artifact replay is exactly what loopback cannot prove; the close does not wait for it.
