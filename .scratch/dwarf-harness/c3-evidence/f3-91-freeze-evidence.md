## Before freeze: PM answers 1–3 and rebound citations

S1 asked three questions on shapes the brief and ruling leave open, and the project manager answered them before the code was final. (1) A successful-call observation may satisfy several causes, so it omits `completion.permit` and requires `completion_permits_object`, while every successful-call state source (`service.complete`, or `service.clear` with a successful-call source, with its cause and membership records) requires `permit` as that cause's captured entry, which S2's consumer checks against the object. (2) A failure observation carries `cause_id`, `cause_key` and `cause`, a successful-call observation none of them, and a deterministic clear or withdraw observation `cause_id` alone. (3) `grace_expiry` is never an observation or `service.record` source, only `service.expire`'s, whose due arithmetic S2's replay checks. The F3-91 section states all three (`contract/ledger/doc.go:3650-3660` on `main`), `TestServiceObservationPresence` pins both sides of answers 1 and 3 and of answer 2's success and clear/withdraw clauses, `TestServiceFrozenFailurePresence` refuses a failure observation without its cause members or with a permits object, and the coder lists them as its three deviations. The ruling predates F3-90's freeze, so its citations were rebound: F3-90 holds schema 8, so F3-91's submission is schema 9, and `Defined()`'s base is 63 rather than the ruling's 61; S1 adds exactly five, and no earlier family or internal API changed.
