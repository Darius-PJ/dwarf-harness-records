# Ticket 143 — implementation design brief

## Owner summary
**Recommendation, pending the advisor's ruling:** keep no settled aggregate for an unconfigured account. Retain its real holds/fences, apply settlement replacement only to configured aggregates, and always reconstruct version-0 budget totals through the existing explicit cold query. This fixes the refund without adding a history scan to correction or ordinary settlement, and also fixes the present-but-incomplete task budget read (§0). No new authority, format, dependency or live call is proposed.

### 0.1 Installation, pruning and reopen

`paidScopes` includes the optional task and sorted/de-duplicated grant ancestors alongside colony, agent and credential (`internal/core/agents/paid_budget.go:60-70`). `paidAuthority` deliberately allows a version-0 **task** account; other unconfigured kinds deny admission (`paid_budget.go:88-98`). Thus a task may participate in accounting without ever receiving spend terms.

`planPaidMoney` produces a copied account per admission scope. `installPaid` assigns those accounts and always calls `prunePaid`; pruning deletes an account whenever `version == 0`, held is zero, and fences are zero, **regardless of settled cost** (`internal/core/agents/paid_budget.go:122-149`; `internal/core/agents/paid_state.go:72-74,109-141`). The initial reservation pins its task account through its hold. A successful settlement releases that hold and the same installation drops the task's settled aggregate unless another attempt's hold/fence still pins it. The deletion does not depend on the settled-attempt hot limit.

Installation is also replay's path: `stageCommitLocked` returns the validated installation closure containing `installPaid` (`internal/core/agents/replay.go:462,606-635`); `OpenPaid` first calls `openService`, then sets the requested paid hot limit and prunes again (`internal/core/agents/paid_live.go:58-79`). Reopening therefore cannot restore an enduring version-0 aggregate: replay already prunes it at each committed installation, and the final paid-service prune repeats that policy. Unresolved holds/fences never evict; settled attempts, separately, form a bounded hot set (`internal/core/agents/paid_state.go:113-141`). This distinction is essential: keeping the target attempt hot does not keep its unconfigured account.

### 0.2 Downward correction and its refusal

`CorrectPaidSettlement` delegates to `recordPaidEvidence`. It resolves the original attempt, verifies the trusted context-owned observation and selected outputs outside `Service.mu`, then re-enters the typed history preparation phase, reloads/deduplicates and derives the replacement result. Preflight calls the same `planPaid`/`planPaidAttempt` reducer used for committed acceptance (`internal/core/agents/paid_settlement.go:258-445`; `internal/core/agents/paid_live.go:239-258`).

`paidResultState` validates exact latest predecessor sequence/evidence, version, provider-message identity, monotone output and historical pricing. It returns the same attempt with replacement `cost`, rather than a caller-provided refund (`internal/core/agents/paid_attempt.go:277-330`). `planPaidAttempt` then calls `planPaidMoney(prior, after, currentPeriod)` (`internal/core/agents/paid_attempt.go:268-275`). For every admission scope in that period, today's money plan computes `settled - prior.cost + after.cost`, adjusts hold/fence deltas, and refuses any negative component (`internal/core/agents/paid_budget.go:122-149`). It assumes a complete ledger-derived settled base is cached in every account.

For the reported same-period `120 → 29` case, the task account was pruned, so its zero base becomes `0 - 120 + 29 = -91`. That triggers `ErrInvalid` at `paid_budget.go:145-146` before Submit; configured scopes retain 120 because the entire group is refused. The ticket records this observed defect and its presence before ticket 142 (`.scratch/dwarf-harness/issues/143-let-a-downward-paid-correction-succeed-when-an-admission-names-an-unconfigured-spend-scope.md:12`). An upward correction can instead leave a positive but incomplete version-0 subtotal; another live hold can leave a partial account even before correction. Neither “map entry exists” nor “the target attempt is hot” proves aggregate completeness.

### 0.4 Fresh replay, configured and unconfigured scopes

There are two different objects to compare, not two authorities:

1. **Full ledger financial fold** (`paidHistoryTotal`): for the requested scope, reconstruct all matching attempts at the selected prefix, replace each result with its latest valid correction, and sum each attempt's final cost if its **admission period** matches. Sum every still-held attempt's full quote across periods. Scope membership is taken from immutable admissions; configuration is not a filter. Thus in the reported one-attempt ledger, the committed original result contributes 120 to **each** admission scope; a committed valid replacement contributes 29 to **each**; after another 12-micro attempt, each shared scope contributes 41. Origin-filtered status restricts that sum to the caller's exact origin (`internal/core/agents/paid_state.go:266-328`). These are arithmetic examples, not new tariff facts.
2. **Fresh service projection**: configured accounts preserve these current-period aggregates through installations. A version-0 account survives only while holds/fences pin it and currently stores an incomplete segment since its last pruning; otherwise it is absent. Owner status hides the missing-account case with the cold fold but not the present/partial case. Replaying a manually committed same-period downward correction currently reaches the same negative-base refusal; this is not permission to weaken historical validation (`internal/core/agents/paid_budget.go:122-149`; `paid_state.go:109-141`; `paid_query.go:278-285`).

The remedy must therefore distinguish **retained live exposure** from **complete historical totals**, fix both the refund and present-account read, and keep all consumer-visible totals equal to the ledger fold after installation and reopening. Ticket 13 explicitly intended no historical aggregate cache for an unconfigured task; its ruling permits labelled historical totals/cold correction and reactivation work, not ordinary whole-history scans (`.scratch/dwarf-harness/c3-evidence/ticket-13-design-brief.md:379`; `ticket-13-design-ruling.md:25`; `docs/DECISIONS.md:672-678`, B3).

## §1 — Numbered design choices
### P1 — Refund planning without an unconfigured settled cache

**Options considered.**

1. Retain every nonzero version-0 settled account. This makes the subtraction possible, but retains accounts for every historically used unconfigured task, violating ticket 13's intended retention policy. A bounded LRU alone is insufficient: a miss or an account retained only by a newer hold still needs an exact complete base before applying a refund.
2. Reconstruct a complete scope aggregate before correction. Correctness is possible using `paidHistoryTotal`, but doing it for every ordinary result/admission with a version-0 account adds whole-history work to an ordinary path; correcting only a missing account misses the present/partial case. A correction-only aggregate witness is avoidable work, and does not by itself fix status.
3. **Recommended: do not cache version-0 settled totals at all.** They authorize no cap. Preserve all held/fence deltas, clear their settled cache, and apply `-before.cost + after.cost` only for configured accounts and only in the admission period. Public totals for version zero come from the ledger fold (P2).

**Reason and cost.** Option 3 matches the original “unconfigured task scope ... needs no historical aggregate cache,” preserves `prunePaid` byte-for-byte, and adds only a constant branch per already-visited scope. No new I/O, scope registry, index, pinned historical accounts or process is introduced. The existing complete incarnation directory and typed cold protocol remain the only history mechanisms; correction still uses existing indexed attempt/route reconstruction when its attempt is cold. Configured money arithmetic remains unchanged; version-0 totals become explicitly unavailable *in the cache*, never semantically zero. Sources: `.scratch/dwarf-harness/c3-evidence/ticket-13-design-brief.md:379-390`; `.scratch/dwarf-harness/c3-evidence/ticket-13-design-ruling.md:24-25,60`; `internal/core/agents/paid_budget.go:88-149`; `internal/core/agents/paid_state.go:113-141,170-263`; `docs/DECISIONS.md:672-678` (B3).

### P2 — Read completeness, cold label and enrollment

**Options considered:** trust any existing version-0 account; add a per-account completeness flag/generation plus cache filling/invalidations; or **always cold-fold version-0 budget status, even when an account exists**.

**Recommend the last.** Extend the existing cold-total condition in `paidBudgetStatus` with `account.version == 0`. Reuse `paidHistoryTotal`/`paidTotalHistoryKey` and its `needPaidTotal` preparation unchanged. Bound-agent origin filtering, historical relatedness, terms visibility, task availability and error identity are unchanged. First task configuration already reconstructs exact usage and held totals and must remain as built. Label this cost **`unconfigured-scope budget status (needPaidTotal)`** in the paid cost paragraph and retained verification notes; this is a documentation/evidence label, not a new record, metric, telemetry service or error field.

**Reason and cost.** A completeness flag would add state, transitions and invalidation merely to reconstruct a deliberately uncached value. Version zero already describes that boundary exactly. An owner current-period read with a live version-0 account becomes an explicit cold query; missing-account reads, historical reads and every bound-agent read were already cold. Its existing fold is one selected-scope whole-history pass, with operation-local matching-attempt/route memory; that temporary cold work is not a permanently retained paid projection. No claim of constant-time/RSS or measured capacity follows. There is no new scan in admission, dispatch, settlement, correction or replay. Sources: `internal/core/agents/paid_query.go:251-315`; `internal/core/agents/paid_plan.go:209-220`; `internal/core/agents/paid_state.go:264-328`; `internal/core/agents/history_prepare.go:149-270`; `internal/core/agents/doc.go:861-872`; `.scratch/dwarf-harness/issues/143-let-a-downward-paid-correction-succeed-when-an-admission-names-an-unconfigured-spend-scope.md:32`.

### P3 — Regression boundary and proof strategy

**Options considered:** only directly append a correction as existing money tests do; only test a hot public correction; or **public prepare/admit/dispatch/settle/correct rows plus one independent committed-ledger replay row**, with overlap and first-enrollment edges.

**Recommend the last.** The first is insufficient for criterion 5's missing admission condition; the second misses the present/partial read, recovery and erased historical allowance. Keep one task-bound fixture helper local to the new test file, using existing work-context/task/grant and real loopback helpers; do not add a production seam or change default fixtures for unrelated tests. The concrete rows and literal oracles are §3. The committed-ledger row deliberately bypasses preflight to prove reopening an already committed valid downward correction—not merely that the live public call succeeds.

Sources: `.scratch/dwarf-harness/issues/143-let-a-downward-paid-correction-succeed-when-an-admission-names-an-unconfigured-spend-scope.md:12,31-38`; `internal/core/agents/paid_task_test.go:19-50`; `internal/core/agents/paid_money_test.go:23-74`; `internal/core/agents/history_index_test.go:1169-1200,1577-1595`; `.scratch/dwarf-harness/c3-evidence/ticket-13-design-ruling.md:26,45-48`.

### P4 — One atomic slice, with local shared-state race evidence

**Options considered:** split arithmetic and status into two separately reviewed slices; or **one slice containing both, their documentation and their regressions**.

**Recommend one.** Either half alone leaves a ticket-criterion violation: the refund would succeed while a pinned version-0 status reports zero, or status would be exact while the refund still refuses. This is a bounded single-package change with no signature or callsite migration. The slice changes how shared money projection state is planned/read, so require package race and affected-row ×20 race despite unchanged lock primitives. One fresh slice review and final exact-SHA three-OS CI remain required; no local full `tools/check`. ### 2.2 Equality and retained-state invariant

For a scope `s`, period `p` and accepted prefix `L`, let `T(s,p,L)` be the sum of the **latest** recognized cost of every attempt whose immutable scopes include `s` and whose admission period is `p`; let `H(s,L)` be the sum of full quotes of still-held attempts, irrespective of admission period. This is the existing ledger fold's behavior, not a new financial rule (`internal/core/agents/paid_state.go:266-328`; `.scratch/dwarf-harness/c3-evidence/ticket-13-design-ruling.md:23-25`).

- **Configured accounts:** existing replacement delta maintains `T` for the current period. Historical-period queries use the cold fold. A first task configuration initializes from `T` and `H` before installing terms, so configuration does not reset usage.
- **Version-0 accounts:** stored settled cache is zero/unusable, but stored `held` and `fences` remain exact. Every returned scope budget total comes from `T`/`H`, even while an account exists. Once held/fences are zero, unchanged pruning removes it. Attempt status still reports that attempt's latest actual cost, independent of scope-cache residency.
- **Live, after correction, after reopen:** all consumers return the same latest cost and per-scope totals. Reopening reduces identical ledger facts with the same configured/unconfigured distinction and never consults artifacts to decide money. No history-sized settled-account retention, no extra retired-profile/grant pins, no changed hot-result limit. The projection's retained-state bound is unchanged; the existing cold query has temporary history-proportional work explicitly described in P2.
- **Periods and unsupported evidence:** old-period corrections change the ledger-derived old-period total, not the new period's configured cache; unresolved full holds cross periods. Unsupported financial evidence retains holds and established charges as before. The fix neither clamps a negative configured aggregate nor bypasses a predecessor/evidence/version/identity check.

Sources: `internal/core/agents/paid_plan.go:209-220`; `internal/core/agents/paid_budget.go:71-149`; `internal/core/agents/paid_state.go:113-141,266-328`; `internal/core/agents/paid_attempt.go:277-335`; `internal/core/agents/paid_query.go:63-90,251-315`; `.scratch/dwarf-harness/c3-evidence/ticket-13-evidence.md:24,99`.

### 2.3 Cold protocol, labels and unchanged consumers

No new cold reader is designed. `PaidBudgetStatus → queryHistory → prepareHistoryLocked → paidHistoryTotal → readHistory(needPaidTotal)` already binds the scope, period, copied optional origin and exact prefix. The resolver owns only immutable selectors and cold-fold locals. It runs outside `Service.mu` for the existing first two suspension rounds; after relock, changed prefix/directory generation/binding discards stale answers and reruns the whole pure query. The documented third-and-later-round locked fallback remains unchanged. Preserve exact read/cancellation/corruption errors; never return partial zero totals after failure. Nothing from the read set is a durable authority or an installed historical account (`internal/core/agents/scoped_query.go:12-54`; `internal/core/agents/history_prepare.go:149-270`; `internal/core/agents/paid_state.go:264-328`; `internal/core/agents/doc.go:431-453`).

P2's added trigger is labelled **`unconfigured-scope budget status (needPaidTotal)`**. No new cold work is inserted into `planPaidMoney` or Open.

Upward corrections still replace once; configured ceilings, refusals, warnings, disclosure, task-version-0 availability and fences retain their current policies. Neither receipt shapes nor submission hashes, evidence encodings, route/profile/tariff lookup, authority, physical budget accounting or private object selection changes (§0 census).
