# Ticket 13 design ruling (advisor AdvisorT13Ruling, 2026-10-25)

Ruling on `local://t13-brief.md` (DesignerT13, at `main` 8ad40fa; at the close both land under `.scratch/dwarf-harness/c3-evidence/` as `ticket-13-design-brief.md` and `ticket-13-design-ruling.md`). **Verdict:** approve-with-changes.

The brief is faithful to T13:35–40 and :62, RC:430–443 and :462, RT §5.3/§9.4/§11, D52, D112, D140, D144 and R11 P9–P11. Of the 15 choices, 11 are accepted and 4 modified; none is rejected. The modifications remove risk or weight: (1) `Open` reads no paid artifact bytes, so ticket 13 cannot recreate ticket 12's S4 regression (finding 1); (2) the zero-settlement rule becomes a fact of the dated, hashed, expiring profile instead of a constant in code (finding 3); (3) one clock seam and one floor, not two (finding 5); (4) fresh-ID proof by per-space watermarks and retired profile digests pinned live, so no ordinary or configuration path scans history (finding 6). The F3-11 reading the brief asks to be recorded is supplied word for word (finding 2). The owner's gates are OG-LIVE and OG-MODEL-FACTS, one combined, optional ask; nothing else is his.
